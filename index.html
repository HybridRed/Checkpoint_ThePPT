Calendar

Add date ranges to the schedule: testing blocks, validation days, holidays, or your own markers such as a code freeze or training week. Use "Add range", or drag across the day headers on the schedule. Weekends never appear.

Schedule

Drag a card to any weekday in its own row, or back to "Not scheduled"; nothing else moves. The × on a card removes it from that row only. Drag across the day headers to add a range. Days over the planned testing hours turn red; cards in the wrong order get a red outline and a "Check" tag.

Needs attention

    Items

    Rows are in priority order (used by Auto-arrange). The coloured letters show which rows an item has a card in; click one to add or remove that card. "Waits for" holds item IDs: D3 = after D3 is delivered, D3:T after it is tested, D3:G after its green flag, D3:P after approval. These only drive the red outlines; nothing moves on its own.

    Plan settings

    Rows on the schedule

    Rename each row, name the team responsible, or hide a row you don't use. Hidden rows keep their cards; they just aren't shown or checked.

    Item groups

    Groups organise the Items table and set the order Auto-arrange works in.
